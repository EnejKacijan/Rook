# Winter theme

Winter uses blue-white surfaces and a glacier accent in Light. Dark uses deep
blue surfaces and a pale ice accent. Fine-tune includes Glacier, Ice, Aurora,
Twilight and Frost.

Static frost and ice crystals decorate the top of the main tabs and the theme
picker tile. A small snow drift sits at the right edge of Today's main action.
Decorations are outside workout screens, sheets and forms, and are hidden in
forced-colors mode. Warning, error and success colors use shared semantic roles.

## Availability

Winter is available from November 26 through March 19, inclusive, in the
user's local time zone. Its first season starts in 2026. The existing theme
discovery flow labels the first release New and subsequent seasons Back.

Users can choose Winter in Profile → Display → Appearance. Once the season
ends, the app restores the previous year-round theme, appearance and Fine-tune
choices at the next safe moment.
