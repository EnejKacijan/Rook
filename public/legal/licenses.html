Exercise illustrations

Most current exercise illustrations were generated for ROOK using AI and reviewed for exercise identification. This does not mean every image is human-authored or that an illustration is medical or technique instruction.

The Cable Crunch illustration is by Bryl Lim / Workout Guide, licensed under Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0. ROOK recolored and reframed it. The adaptation retains that license. The upstream notices also preserve Everkinetic credits for their relevant source assets; ROOK does not assign that authorship to every current image.

Exercise reference data

Workout Guide provides exercise catalog information, including names, muscles and equipment. Upstream software/documentation notices and visual-asset notices apply to their respective materials; they are not a blanket license for ROOK or its AI replacements.

Fonts and software

The application uses a system font stack, without a remote webfont download. React, Firebase, Capacitor and other included dependencies retain their own licenses. The accompanying software-notices.txt contains the installed production-package license inventory and available notice texts. Server-only packages are identified separately from browser code.

A license inventory does not establish ownership of a trademark or guarantee that all AI output is copyrightable. The operator should retain source/asset records and review any new font, image, SDK or marketing material before adding it.
