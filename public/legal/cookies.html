Cookies & storage

Essential storage by default; optional local diagnostics only with your choice.

Essential storage

ROOK uses localStorage, sessionStorage, IndexedDB and the browser’s service-worker Cache Storage to provide the functions you request: saved training, local photos, offline app files, recovery and backup journals, navigation/settings and privacy choices. These mechanisms can store information even when no traditional cookie is set.

Examples include lift-v2-state for saved training, ROOK profile/recovery namespaces, personal-theme settings, rook-privacy-preferences-v1 and the rook-v* offline cache. Exact keys and recovery entries vary with the selected profile and app version.

Authentication storage

When enabled, Firebase Authentication uses browser/app persistence to keep your selected sign-in session. Google sign-in also opens the provider’s own authentication surface, whose storage is controlled by that provider. ROOK does not describe all third-party authentication storage as tracking-free.

Optional local usage diagnostics

This is off by default. If enabled in Profile → Privacy & legal, lift-funnel-events-v1 stores up to 250 feature-use events; lift-funnel-once-v1 and lift-funnel-session-once-v1 avoid duplicate events. No remote analytics adapter is connected. Turning the choice off removes those events and duplicate-event markers.

There are no advertising cookies, third-party analytics SDKs, remote webfonts or marketing pixels in the current app source. A future optional tracking integration must remain blocked until informed consent is obtained, and rejection must be as easy as acceptance.

Your choices

Essential storage runs to provide the requested app functions. Optional diagnostics do not run before you switch them on, so there is no “accept all” banner in this version. You can change that optional choice in Privacy & legal at any time.

Delete local data removes ROOK data on this device after confirmation. Clearing browser storage also removes training and photos; export a backup first if you wish to keep them. Neither action deletes a cloud account or copies you exported elsewhere.
