Privacy policy

How ROOK uses your data and the choices you control.

Who is responsible

The operator is the controller of personal data processed to provide ROOK. Verified operator and contact details are listed on the Operator & contact page. These notices remain drafts until those details and the operational requirements in the release checklist are completed.

Data on your device

ROOK stores your training profile, plans, completed and active workouts, exercise preferences, weight check-ins and Coach conversations in browser or app storage. Name, age range, sex, restrictions and photos are optional. Do not enter details you do not want processed. A display name does not have to be your legal name.

Workout photos are kept locally in IndexedDB. They are not included in account sync. A backup you explicitly export can include photos. Anyone who has access to an unencrypted backup may be able to read it; keep it somewhere you trust.

Account and cloud backup

When the account feature is enabled, Google Firebase Authentication handles sign-in and Cloud Firestore holds a cloud copy of supported training data. The account system may create a device-specific anonymous identity before you link Google or email. Linking is optional for local workout logging. Firebase processes account identifiers, authentication information and technical connection data. ROOK does not receive your Google password.

Cloud sync includes supported training records, profile preferences and restrictions. It excludes workout photo files and Coach conversations. Signing out or deleting local data does not delete the cloud account.

AI requests and sensitive information

AI sharing is off until you explicitly allow it. When you request an AI feature, ROOK sends the information relevant to that request through its server to OpenAI. This can include your message, imported plan notes, training preferences, restrictions and relevant workout context. Your structured display name, account contact details and display preferences are excluded from AI profile context. Free text is not automatically anonymised: do not include passwords, another person’s data or unnecessary health details.

Restrictions and messages may reveal health information. The AI consent asks separately for explicit permission for this processing. Optional photo analysis sends resized copies only when you choose photos and request their review. Those temporary review copies are not saved into your profile by the photo-review feature.

ROOK requests OpenAI responses with storage disabled. This does not disable all provider processing or safety retention. OpenAI normally retains abuse-monitoring data for up to 30 days, with exceptions; flagged-image and legal retention can differ. The linked OpenAI policy explains the applicable controls. AI output can be inaccurate; you decide whether to apply a proposed workout change.

Usage diagnostics and cookies

Optional usage diagnostics are off by default. If you switch them on, ROOK keeps at most 250 limited feature-use events locally; it does not send them to a remote analytics provider. Messages, photos, names and restriction notes are not analytics properties. Turning diagnostics off clears the local event history and stops further event recording.

Essential storage keeps the training data and settings you ask ROOK to save, protects recovery, and remembers privacy choices. ROOK does not load advertising or third-party analytics SDKs. See Cookies & storage for details.

Purposes and legal bases — proposed for release

Core local training and requested account/backup functions provide the service you choose. Security and abuse controls protect the service. Optional usage diagnostics and optional AI processing rely on your separate choices; explicit consent covers health information you choose to include in AI requests. Consent is not bundled with marketing or required for local logging.

The operator must confirm the lawful bases for cloud processing of health-related restrictions, any international-transfer safeguards and any age/parental-consent requirements before enabling those services publicly. This draft is not evidence that these operational checks have been completed.

Providers and international processing

Netlify hosts the web service and server functions; OpenAI processes AI requests; Google Firebase provides authentication, cloud backup and AI usage-limit records when configured. Hosting and authentication providers can process IP addresses and technical request metadata. No advertising-network or payment SDK is integrated in this release.

Provider processing may take place outside your country, including outside the EEA. The operator must confirm the deployed regions, provider agreements and appropriate transfer safeguards before release. ROOK does not promise EU-only processing or zero retention. Provider privacy information is linked below.

Retention and security

Local training and photos remain until you remove them, clear app/browser storage or lose access to the device. Browser storage is not a guaranteed backup. Exported files remain wherever you save them. Account data remains with the configured cloud service until it is deleted through an account-data request or an applicable retention process.

AI usage controls keep account-linked usage counters and expiring request leases for security and cost limits; they do not store the message body as an application transcript on the server. Hosting/authentication logs and provider backups follow their applicable retention arrangements. Their exact deployed settings must be confirmed before publication.

ROOK uses authenticated cloud ownership checks, authenticated AI requests, input limits and transport security where the service is deployed over HTTPS. These measures do not guarantee that data can never be lost or accessed without permission.

Your choices and rights

You can use local logging without an account, leave optional fields blank, decline AI sharing, and turn local diagnostics off. Withdraw AI consent in Profile → Privacy & legal. Withdrawal stops new AI submissions; it cannot recall requests already sent or erase data under a separate lawful retention obligation.

Depending on applicable law, you may request access, correction, erasure, portability, restriction or object to processing. Use Data access & deletion and the verified operator contact when it is published. You may also complain to the data-protection authority where you live; for Slovenia this is the Information Commissioner (Informacijski pooblaščenec). Requests may require proportionate verification. Do not send a password or a full workout/photo backup to prove identity.

Children and changes

The operator must settle supported ages and any required parental permissions before public release. This draft does not establish a verified child-consent process.

We will update this notice when processing changes. Materially different AI processing requires a new consent version; merely reading this policy does not opt you into optional processing.
