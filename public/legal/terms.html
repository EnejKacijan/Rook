Training and AI limits

Exercise involves risk. Choose movements and loads appropriate to your ability and stop if you experience pain or concerning symptoms. Seek advice from a qualified clinician when an injury, condition or treatment affects exercise. ROOK is not a medical service and does not diagnose, treat or replace personalised clinical advice.

Plans, progression tips, estimated duration, estimated strength metrics and AI replies are guidance. Results vary, and ROOK does not guarantee muscle gain, weight loss, injury prevention or any other outcome. Review suggested changes before applying them. An illustration identifies an exercise and is not a complete demonstration of safe technique.
