Payments & refunds

No payment is collected in this release.

Draft — not ready for public release

Verified operator/contact details and the release checks must be completed before these notices become final.

Current release

ROOK currently has no checkout, paid plan, free-to-paid trial, subscription renewal or payment SDK. It does not collect payment-card details. There is therefore no in-app ROOK purchase to cancel or refund in this version.

If you see a charge

Check the merchant shown by your payment provider and contact the verified operator through Operator & contact when available. Do not send card numbers or banking credentials. We do not claim that a charge from another merchant is a ROOK charge.

Future paid features

A future paid offer must explain total price, billing frequency, cancellation and refund/withdrawal rights before purchase. Store-specific purchase rules may apply if a store processes the payment. This notice does not waive statutory rights or create a blanket “no refunds” rule.
